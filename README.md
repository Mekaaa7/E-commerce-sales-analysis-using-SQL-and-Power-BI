# E-commerce-sales-analysis-using-SQL-and-Power-BI

Project Overview

This project analyzes 10,000 e-commerce transaction records to identify sales trends, product performance, customer behavior, and revenue distribution across different cities and online platforms. SQL was used for data validation, KPI calculations, and business analysis, while Power BI was used to build an interactive dashboard for data visualization and decision-making.

Key Business Insights
Total revenue generated was approximately ₹302 Million from 10,000 orders.
Electronics was the highest revenue-generating category, contributing approximately ₹92 Million in sales.
Xiaomi Redmi Note 12 emerged as the top-performing product with revenue exceeding ₹33 Million.
Cairo recorded the highest city-wise revenue, indicating strong market demand.
Revenue distribution across Souq, Jumia, and Amazon remained relatively balanced, reducing dependence on a single sales channel.
Monthly sales remained stable throughout the year, with July recording the highest revenue and December the lowest.
The average customer rating was 3.01, highlighting opportunities to improve customer satisfaction and product experience.
